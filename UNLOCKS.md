# Assassins in Plain Sight unlocks

Every account starts with **20 gold coins**. Items priced at **0** are available immediately. Other items can be purchased from the **Unlocks** menu at the listed price. There is no match-score requirement. Buying all twelve paid assassins and all seven paid abilities costs **87 gold coins**.

## Abilities

| Ability | Effect | Cooldown | Unlock cost (gold coins) |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| Smoke Cloud | Creates a wide, opaque smoke cloud for 4 seconds. | 15 s | 0 |
| Disguise | Copies a targeted character’s appearance for 15 seconds from up to 12 units away. | 15 s | 0 |
| Firecrackers | Sends citizens within 4 units sprinting for 4 seconds. | 25 s | 2 |
| Invisibility Potion | Makes you invisible and holds stealth at 150 (Incognito) for 6 seconds. You can move, while attacks, stuns, blending, and other abilities are disabled. | 30 s | 3 |
| Cult | Gives citizens within 5 units your current appearance for the rest of the match. | 25 s | 3 |
| Sprint Boost | Doubles walking and running speed for 10 seconds. | 15 s | 1 |
| Decoy | Sends one citizen up to 12 units away sprinting in a random direction for 5 seconds. | 23 s | 3 |
| Mute | Disables a targeted player’s powers and contract compass for 6 seconds from up to 12 units away. | 15 s | 2 |
| Gun | Fires a visible shot at a target up to 30 units away. Stealth drops before kill points are awarded. | 30 s | 1 |

The [game definitions](combat.py) set assassin prices in [roster](dist/game.js) order and define ability prices.
